> ABOUT

HackJam CU is a 12-hour student hackathon hosted on the Chandigarh University campus. It's built for undergrads, first-time hackers, and seasoned builders alike — bring a team of 2 to 4, get a surprise theme on the day, and ship a working prototype before the clock runs out. Mentors, food, and a judging panel are provided; the idea is yours.

01 / BUILD

Any stack, any idea. Web, mobile, hardware or games all count.

02 / LEARN

Mentors walk the floor all day to unblock you.

03 / SHIP

Demo to a live judging panel and win real prizes.

> FAQ

Do I need a team already?

Teams are 2 to 4 people. Register with your team; if you are solo, ask a friend or classmate to join.

Is it beginner friendly?

Yes. There is a Best Beginner Team prize and mentors on the floor all day.

What should I bring?

A laptop, charger, student ID and your best ideas. Food, WiFi and power are on us.

Can I start building before the day?

No. The theme is revealed at kickoff and all code must be written during the 12 hours.
